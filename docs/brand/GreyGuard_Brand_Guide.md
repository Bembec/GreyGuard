# GreyGuard Brand Guide

## Logo system

1. Primary stacked logo: splash screens, login, onboarding and presentations.
2. Horizontal logo: application headers, expanded navigation and documentation headers.
3. Symbol-only mark: favicon, PWA icon, collapsed navigation and compact UI contexts.

The tagline is written exactly as **SECURE • MONITOR • CONTROL**.

## Colour palette

| Role | Hex |
| --- | --- |
| Deep Navy | `#050A12` |
| Security Blue | `#0062C4` |
| Electric Glow | `#48C8F8` |
| Steel Grey | `#8C939A` |
| Light Steel | `#E0E8E8` |

These values are UI tokens derived from the approved direction. Preserve the original artwork colours in the supplied logo files.

## Typography

- Interface and body: **Inter**
- Headings and branded display text: **Saira**
- Self-host the approved font files in production and retain their licence files.

## Clear space and minimum size

- Maintain clear space equal to at least one quarter of the symbol width.
- Do not display the detailed symbol below 32 px; use a separately approved simplified small-size mark when available.
- The horizontal wordmark should normally be at least 160 px wide.
- The tagline may be removed when it becomes unreadable.

## Backgrounds

- The supplied metallic/electric-blue artwork is approved for deep navy and near-black backgrounds.
- Do not place it directly over photographs or visually noisy surfaces.
- A separate approved flat dark mark is required for light backgrounds.

## Prohibited usage

- Do not stretch, skew, rotate or crop through the mark.
- Do not change the wordmark spelling or tagline punctuation.
- Do not add unapproved shadows, glows, gradients or colours.
- Do not use the reference-sheet labels “SYMBOL” or “LOGO” as part of the product logo.
- Do not claim that a raster conversion is an official vector master.
