# Remaining Vector and Print Work

The package is ready for web prototyping and application integration. Before final commercial release, commission and approve:

- True SVG/EPS/PDF vector masters recreated from the approved geometry
- Transparent full-colour horizontal, stacked and symbol variants
- Flat light-background and dark-background variants
- Pure white, pure black and greyscale variants
- A simplified pixel-hinted favicon/small-icon master
- Wordmark-only master
- CMYK and spot-colour print specifications
- Exact logo construction, exclusion-zone and minimum-size measurements
- Licensed self-hosted Inter and Saira font files
- Final social-preview, documentation-cover and presentation templates
- Trademark and distinctiveness review before registration or commercial claims

The original reference images under `source/original-reference-images/` remain the visual authority until vector masters are approved.
