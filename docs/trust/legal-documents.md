## Notes for counsel

- **No licence file.** The repository has no licence file. The Python SDK's package metadata declares its licence as "Proprietary" (`sdk/python/pyproject.toml`).
- **Engineering facts the documents will need** are in the Trust Center source pages, especially [Subprocessors](subprocessors.md), [Privacy, retention and deletion](privacy-retention-deletion.md) and [Availability and recovery](availability-and-recovery.md). Those pages state what the software does today, not what the documents should promise.
